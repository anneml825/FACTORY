## Listing description

**CANDLE COST & PRICING CALCULATOR — GOOGLE SHEETS**

Take the guesswork out of candle costing and pricing with a calculator that brings your recipe,
batch quantities, costs and selling price together in one place.

This Google Sheets candle calculator helps you work out how much wax and fragrance oil you need,
what each candle costs to make, and what you need to charge to reach your target profit margin
after selling fees.

Set it up with your own candle recipe, materials and supplier prices, then reuse it as you make
new batches, update your costs or review your pricing.

**WHAT THIS CALCULATOR HELPS YOU DO**

**Know your true cost per candle**
See the cost of your wax, fragrance oil, vessel, wick, lid, label and packaging together. You can
also include labor and overhead if you want a fuller picture of what it costs your business to
make each candle.

**Price your candles for your target profit margin**
Enter the margin you want and the selling fees that apply to you. The calculator works out the
selling price needed to reach that margin and shows the result alongside your markup.

**Plan your next batch**
Start with your vessel and recipe to calculate the wax and fragrance oil needed for one candle or
a full batch, with an optional allowance for material left behind during pouring.

**Update your numbers as your costs change**
If the price of wax, fragrance oil, packaging or another input changes, update your costs and use
the calculator to review the effect on your cost per candle and pricing.

**Check your wholesale pricing**
See your wholesale price alongside the corresponding reseller retail price, so you can review
whether your current costs and pricing work for wholesale.

**ONE CONNECTED CALCULATOR**

Instead of working out your recipe quantities, product costs and pricing separately, this
template keeps the calculations together.

Enter your own information and use it to calculate:

• Wax and fragrance oil per candle
• Wax and fragrance oil for a full batch
• Fragrance load and fragrance content
• Material cost per candle
• Optional labor and overhead
• Total cost per candle
• Target selling price
• Selling fees
• Amount received after fees
• Profit margin and markup
• Wholesale pricing

**FRAGRANCE LOAD & FRAGRANCE CONTENT**

The calculator keeps fragrance load and fragrance content separate because they are two different
measurements.

For example, 20 g of fragrance oil with 200 g of wax equals a 10% fragrance load but 9.09%
fragrance content.

Choose the measurement your recipe uses and the calculator displays both figures, helping you
avoid accidentally treating one percentage as the other.

**PROFIT MARGIN & MARKUP**

Margin and markup are also calculated separately.

For example, adding a 40% markup to your cost does not produce a 40% profit margin.

Instead of making you work backwards from your costs, the calculator lets you enter the profit
margin you want and calculates the selling price needed to reach it using the selling fees you
enter.

**USE GRAMS OR OUNCES**

Choose grams or ounces in the calculator and work in the unit you normally use for your candle
recipes and supplies. Both are included in the same template.

**WHAT YOU RECEIVE**

• Candle Cost & Pricing Calculator for Google Sheets
• Printable Burn Test Log
• A reference tab inside the calculator explaining its terms, assumptions and editable settings
• Instructions for making your own copy and getting started

**WHO IT'S FOR**

Designed for candle makers who want a simpler way to manage the numbers behind making and selling
their candles.

It can be especially useful if you're starting a candle business, reviewing your current pricing,
planning production batches, updating prices after your costs change, or exploring wholesale
pricing.

**PLEASE NOTE**

This template is designed for Google Sheets and requires a free Google account. It is not an Excel
template.

Selling fees are entered by you so you can use the rates that currently apply to your marketplace
or payment method.

The water-to-wax factor is an editable planning assumption because different waxes can vary.

This calculator does not select or recommend wick sizes. Wick performance needs to be tested with
your specific wax, fragrance and vessel. The included Burn Test Log gives you a place to record
those tests.

**DIGITAL DOWNLOAD**

This is a digital product. No physical item will be shipped.

After purchase, follow the included instructions to make your own copy of the Google Sheets
template and replace the sample information with your candle recipe, costs and pricing.
