ETSY DESCRIPTION

CRAFT FAIR & FARMERS MARKET PROFIT TRACKER

You made sales. But was the market actually worth it?

A busy booth and a big sales number can feel like a great market day. But once you factor in the booth fee, travel, product costs and the time you put in, a different event may have actually performed better.

The Craft Fair & Farmers Market Profit Tracker helps you see the difference.

Enter what you sold, what it cost you and how much time you spent. Then compare your markets side by side to see what was left after the costs you entered, how much stock you sold, what you made per market hour and which events reached your own goals.

FIND OUT WHICH MARKETS ACTUALLY PERFORMED BETTER

The highest-sales market isn’t always the strongest one.

The included sample data shows exactly that:

Riverside Pop-Up
$478 sales → $182 profit after entered costs → $36.40 per market hour

Night Bazaar
$509 sales → $51 profit after entered costs → $6.38 per market hour

More sales. Much less left behind.

Instead of judging an event by the sales total alone, you can compare the numbers that matter to your business.

TRACK THE COSTS BEHIND THE SALES

For each product batch, enter:

• Units brought
• Units sold
• Sales
• Ingredient costs
• Packaging costs
• Product work hours
• The value you assign to your work time

The workbook also factors in each product row’s share of the booth fee and travel cost you entered for that event.

The result is Profit After Entered Costs—a consistent way to compare one market with another using your own numbers.

SEE WHAT YOUR TIME PRODUCED

Selling for eight hours and making your products for five hours are two different things, so the workbook tracks them separately.

Hours Worked at Market shows how much Profit After Entered Costs the market produced for each hour you spent working the event.

Product Work Hours lets you include the value of the time you spent making or preparing a product batch in its entered costs.

Both are clearly labeled so you know exactly what goes where.

SET YOUR OWN GOALS

You decide what a worthwhile market looks like for your business.

Set your minimum:

• Profit Margin
• Profit per Market Hour
• Stock Sold %

When an event reaches all three, you’ll see MEETS TARGETS.

If one or more falls short, you’ll see REVIEW.

No universal rules about whether you should book a market again—the workbook compares the event against the goals you chose.

COMPARE UP TO 5 MARKETS SIDE BY SIDE

Use Market Comparison to quickly review:

• Sales
• Profit After Entered Costs
• Profit Margin
• Units Sold
• Stock Sold %
• Profit per Market Hour
• Target status

And use the Dashboard to see your overall sales, profit after entered costs, margin, unsold stock and market results together.

USE IT AGAIN AFTER EVERY MARKET

1. Set your three goals.
2. Log the market’s booth fee, travel cost and hours worked.
3. Enter what you brought, sold and spent on each product batch.
4. Review your results.
5. Compare markets and see which ones worked best for you.

Built-in input checks flag missing, invalid, duplicate or unmatched entries so incomplete rows don’t quietly flow into your totals.

MADE FOR

Craft fair vendors, farmers market sellers, makers and other physical-product businesses that want to know more than “How much did I sell?”

Use the same process after every event and start seeing which markets are actually giving you the strongest results for the costs and time you put in.

IMPORTANT SCOPE

Profit After Entered Costs uses only the costs and time value you enter. It isn’t bookkeeping profit, net income, take-home pay or a tax result and doesn’t include every possible business expense.
