CRAFT FAIR & FARMERS MARKET PROFIT TRACKER

Open the .xlsx file in desktop Microsoft Excel and begin on Start Here.

Use the sheets in this order:
1. Targets — enter your three decision goals.
2. Market Days — enter one row per event.
3. Product Sales — enter one row per product batch sold at that event.
4. Market Comparison — enter up to five market names to compare.
5. Dashboard — review the overall and market-by-market results.

Blue cells are buyer inputs. Green cells are calculated. Yellow/red cells need attention.

Important definitions:
• Hours Worked at Market is time spent working the event.
• Product Work Hours is time spent making or preparing a product batch.
• Profit After Entered Costs uses only the costs and time value entered in this workbook. It is not accounting profit, take-home pay or a tax result.
• MEETS TARGETS means all three displayed results reached your displayed goals. REVIEW means at least one did not.

The workbook ships with sample data so you can see how it works. It is marked at the top of
each sheet that contains it: Market Days (rows 5-7), Product Sales (rows 5-10) and Market
Comparison (the market names). Delete all of it before entering your own information, or your
results will combine your figures with the samples.

File: editable .xlsx workbook, no macros. Designed for desktop Microsoft Excel. Google Sheets compatibility is not included or supported.
