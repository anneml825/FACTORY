# Nursing one-pagers

One-page condition study sheets for nursing students. A series format: each high-yield
condition is its own product, and bundles follow naturally.

**Niche evidence (OBSERVED, supply-side only):** Etsy listings for diabetes and insulin
nursing cheat sheets run from `841938185` (≈2020) to `4368056205` (≈2026), and a
`nursing_cheat_sheet` market page exists. Purchase volume is UNKNOWN.

## Sheets

| Condition | Status |
|---|---|
| Heart Failure | Built, one US Letter page, visually QA'd. **Needs clinical review before sale.** |

## Clinical-review requirement

Content is written from the 2022 AHA/ACC/HFSA Heart Failure Guideline and standard
nursing-education material, **from model knowledge**. The primary guideline could not be
opened this session — web fetch is blocked by the egress proxy — so no line has been checked
against the source document.

Before any sheet is sold, a licensed nurse or nurse educator should read it once against a
current reference. Errors in study material propagate into what students learn.

Deliberately excluded: drug doses, lab reference ranges beyond the one widely taught BNP
threshold, and any individualized clinical decision.
