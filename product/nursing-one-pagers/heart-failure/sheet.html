HEART FAILURE

The heart cannot pump enough blood to meet the body's needs, so blood backs up (congestion) and organs are underperfused.

Left-sided HF

L = LUNGS  ·  backs up into the pulmonary circulation
  • Dyspnea, orthopnea (needs more pillows), PND — waking breathless at night
  • Crackles in the lungs; cough
  • Pink, frothy sputum = pulmonary edema — emergency
  • Tachycardia, S3 heart sound
  • Fatigue, weakness, activity intolerance
  • Restlessness, confusion — ↓ cerebral perfusion
  • ↓ Urine output — ↓ renal perfusion

Right-sided HF

R = REST OF THE BODY  ·  backs up into systemic veins
  • JVD — jugular vein distension
  • Dependent edema — legs and ankles; sacrum if bedbound
  • Weight gain from fluid retention
  • Hepatomegaly, RUQ discomfort; ascites
  • Anorexia, nausea, bloating — GI congestion
  • Most common cause of right-sided HF: left-sided HF

Diagnostics

  • BNP / NT-proBNP — released when ventricles stretch; elevated in HF. BNP <100 pg/mL makes HF unlikely
  • Echocardiogram — measures EF; the key test
  • Chest x-ray — cardiomegaly, pulmonary congestion
  • Electrolytes, renal function — watch K⁺ and creatinine on diuretics and RAAS drugs

Nursing priorities

  • Daily weight — same time, same scale, same clothing, after voiding, before breakfast
  • Report a gain of 2–3 lb in a day or 5 lb in a week
  • High Fowler's for dyspnea; oxygen as ordered
  • Strict I&O; sodium and fluid restriction as prescribed
  • Assess lung sounds, edema, JVD, and mental status every shift
  • Cluster care; plan rest periods; increase activity gradually

⚠ Acute pulmonary edema

  • Severe dyspnea, pink frothy sputum, crackles throughout, anxiety, hypoxia
  • High Fowler's with legs dependent; oxygen
  • Call the provider or rapid response
  • Prepare IV loop diuretic as ordered

Patient teaching

  • Weigh daily; report the gain thresholds above
  • Follow the prescribed sodium limit
  • Avoid NSAIDs — they worsen fluid retention
  • Report more breathlessness, swelling, or needing more pillows to sleep
