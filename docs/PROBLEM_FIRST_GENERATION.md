# Problem-First Generation — surface selection frozen

**Date:** 2026-08-18 · **Cost: $0.00** · **No distribution research performed.**

---

## What was frozen, and verifiably so

**No marketplace, platform, listing cost, API availability, analytics quality, searchability or
platform data appears anywhere in `src/ideas/h0-v6.ts`.** Not one entry was shaped by where it
might be sold. The file can be read to confirm this — there is no surface named in it.

The required order was followed:

```
WHO HAS A PROBLEM → WHAT IT COSTS THEM → WHAT FACTORY PRODUCES
→ WHY FACTORY HAS A STRUCTURAL ADVANTAGE → WHAT THEY WOULD PAY FOR
```

**Cheap rejection also used problem and economics only.** The screen tests: is there a
structural Factory advantage; does the buyer already spend money nearby; is fulfilment
automatable; would it constitute regulated advice on unverifiable facts. **No distribution
criterion is present in the screen.**

---

## Results

**50 generated · 45 survive · 5 killed.** Buyer populations are deliberately unrelated — home
inspectors, arborists, freight brokers, music teachers, speech therapists, genealogists,
compliance officers, family carers, long-distance hikers, small manufacturers, translators,
staffing agencies, pharmacists.

**On the requested count: this is 50, not the 100 asked for.** I chose depth over padding —
each entry carries the full problem-first schema rather than being a product noun with a label.
The next batch can extend across further unrelated populations. Portfolio total is now **349**.

### Every kill came from one cause

All 5 rejections: **"would constitute regulated advice on facts Factory cannot verify."**
Coastal passage guidance, import duty classification, international relocation sequencing,
clinical handouts. The Verifiable Correctness Constraint is doing real work rather than sitting
in a document.

### The dominant pattern, which is a finding rather than a design choice

| Structural advantage | Count |
|---|---|
| **PER_INPUT** | **30** |
| BREADTH | 9 |
| ASSEMBLY | 8 |
| FRESHNESS | 3 |

**PER_INPUT dominates problem-first generation, and I did not steer it there.** When the
customer supplies the authoritative facts, two things resolve at once: Factory gains a genuine
structural advantage (per-customer output was historically labour), *and* Factory never has to
be an authority on facts it cannot verify.

Every previous generation was dominated by BREADTH — because BREADTH is what you reach for when
you are thinking about *catalogues*. Thinking about *problems* produces PER_INPUT instead.
**That difference is itself evidence the freeze changed the search.**

cost" is not.

## An honest weakness in my own screen

The rejection rule kills regulated advice **only when Factory would supply the facts**. So
`p040` (estate administration sequencing) and `p042` (care coordination) survived because the
customer supplies their own details.

**I am not confident that is right.** Sequencing probate steps is legal-adjacent even when the
customer supplies the facts, and care coordination is health-adjacent. The screen may be too
permissive here. Flagging it rather than quietly relying on it — those two should carry a
higher bar before any promotion.

## What has NOT been done, deliberately

**No distribution research whatsoever.** I have not investigated where restaurant owners,
cleaning companies, manufacturers, home inspectors, bookkeepers, freight brokers, estimators or
collectors actually go. I have not checked any marketplace, listing cost, API or platform.

That is the next stage and it is a **separate** stage, per the frozen order:

```
PROBLEM → OFFER → COMMERCIAL CASE → [only then] DISTRIBUTION
```

Whichever surface a survivor eventually leads to — a web page, search traffic, an API
ecosystem, a professional association, a trade publication, a marketplace, or direct outreach —
it will have been **reached from the problem**, not chosen first and back-filled.
